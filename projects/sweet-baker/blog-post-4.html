How to Keep Cakes and Cookies Fresh

By Sweet & Baker • August 2026 • 5 min read

Freshly baked cakes and cookies are delicious, but proper storage is important if you want to keep their texture, flavour and appearance at their best. A few simple storage habits can help your favourite treats stay enjoyable for longer.

1. Let Baked Goods Cool Completely

Before storing cakes or cookies, allow them to cool properly. Storing warm baked goods can trap moisture and affect their texture.

2. Store Cake in an Airtight Container

Keeping cake covered helps reduce exposure to air. An appropriate airtight container can help maintain the cake's texture and prevent it from becoming unnecessarily dry.

3. Keep Cookies in a Sealed Container

Cookies are best protected from air and moisture when stored in a suitable sealed container. Keeping them covered can help maintain their texture and flavour.

4. Keep Different Cookies Separate

Different types of cookies may have different textures and flavours. Storing strongly flavoured cookies separately can help prevent flavours from mixing.

5. Avoid Unnecessary Exposure to Air

Leaving cakes and cookies uncovered for long periods can affect their freshness. Keep them covered when they are not being served.

6. Store According to the Dessert

Different desserts have different storage needs. Some cakes and desserts may require refrigeration, especially when they contain ingredients that need to be kept chilled.

7. Keep Your Desserts Away from Heat

Heat can affect the texture and appearance of baked goods. Store cakes and cookies in an appropriate cool and dry place when refrigeration is not needed.

8. Keep Decoration Looking Fresh

Carefully storing decorated cakes can help protect their appearance. Avoid placing heavy items on top of decorated desserts.

9. Serve Only What You Need

If you are serving desserts over a period of time, keeping the remaining portions covered can help protect them while they are waiting to be served.

10. Enjoy Your Desserts Fresh

The best way to enjoy cakes and cookies is to serve them when they are fresh and at their best. Good storage simply helps protect their quality until it is time to enjoy them.

Final Thoughts

Proper storage does not have to be complicated. Keeping baked goods covered, protected from unnecessary exposure to air and stored appropriately can help maintain their quality.
